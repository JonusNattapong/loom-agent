# V0.8 architecture note: Remote Workers & Network A2A

V0.8 should extend the durable V0.7 job/lease model rather than replace it. The first slice should be coordinator/worker separation over an outbound persistent connection. Workers initiate connectivity to the coordinator, advertise capabilities and load, and receive authenticated leased jobs.

## Proposed flow

```text
worker → outbound WebSocket/HTTP2 connection → coordinator
                                      ↓
                         registry + policy routing
                                      ↓
                         remote job lease/dispatch
```

The existing capability-aware router remains the policy authority. A model may recommend a role, but worker selection, trust, permissions, leases, and replay remain runtime decisions. V0.7 `jobs`, `rootAgentId`, task leases, idempotency ledger, and recovery records should be reused as the durable foundation.

## Initial scope

- authenticated worker registration and heartbeat
- capability/role/tool advertisement
- coordinator policy routing
- outbound persistent worker connection
- remote job dispatch and lease renewal
- reconnect/replay after worker disconnect
- worker trust boundaries and explicit approval propagation

Do not begin with a peer-to-peer mesh, inbound ports, NAT traversal, or direct worker-to-worker links. Reverse tunnel first; relay/overlay and NAT traversal can follow only after coordinator/worker semantics are proven. Distributed consensus and high-availability claims remain out of scope.
